# Research worker output contract

## Question
Exact bounded question.

## Conclusion
Shortest evidence-supported answer. If unresolved, say unresolved.

## Confidence
high | medium | low

## Evidence
For each item: source/location, observation, why it bears on the question.

## Contradictions
Conflicting evidence or `None found`.

## Unknowns
Facts still needed or `None`.

## Implications for plan
Only consequences directly supported by the evidence. Do not write the plan.
